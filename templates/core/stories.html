Shabnam Begum: Turning Challenges into Change

Married at 16 and forced to leave school, Shabnam continued her education and became a grassroots reporter. She later founded Nazar Ekta Foundation and organized women and marginalized families to access education, livelihoods, public services, and their rights.

Farida Khan: Finding Her Voice Through Education

Married at a young age and divorced before turning 16, Farida had not received a formal school education. She began studying at a community learning centre, completed further education, earned a B.Ed., and went on to help other women and girls access learning opportunities.
