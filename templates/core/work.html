Our Work

Programs that create opportunities

Nayaa works with children, young people, women, families, and communities to create opportunities for learning, health, empowerment, skills, livelihoods, and community development.

Education

Nayaa works to improve access to quality education and learning opportunities for children and young people.

Our education initiatives encourage learning, skill development, confidence, and opportunities for children to build a better future.

Healthcare

Nayaa promotes healthcare awareness and encourages healthier communities through knowledge, awareness, and community-focused initiatives.

We aim to help families and communities understand the importance of health, wellbeing, prevention, and access to essential support.

Women Empowerment

Nayaa supports women in developing confidence, useful skills, independence, and opportunities to participate actively in their communities.

Our initiatives focus on creating pathways that encourage self-reliance, dignity, and economic opportunities for women.

Skills & Livelihood

Nayaa encourages young people and community members to develop useful skills that can support their personal and professional futures.

We create pathways towards skill development, livelihood opportunities, economic participation, and greater self-reliance.

Community Development

Nayaa works to strengthen communities through awareness, participation, inclusion, and community-focused activities.

We encourage people to participate in decisions that affect their lives and work together to create healthier, stronger, and more inclusive communities.

Next Generation

Nayaa focuses on creating opportunities for the next generation by bringing education, healthcare, empowerment, skills, livelihoods, and community development together.

Through these efforts, we aim to encourage equality, inclusion, dignity, confidence, and long-term positive social impact.

Our Approach

Nayaa believes that meaningful social impact begins with creating equal opportunities. Our work brings together education, healthcare, women empowerment, skill development, livelihood opportunities, and community development to support people in building a better future.

We aim to encourage inclusion, dignity, participation, confidence, self-reliance, and long-term positive change for children, young people, women, families, and communities.
